This list comes from the [Skate 3 Level Loader](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skate3-level-loader/tree/master/catalog) catalog: 43 known packs with 134 maps, each load-tested on the [Skate 3 Recomp](/guides/skate-3-recomp/). "Boots" means the map loaded. "Stalls" means it hung on load in automatic mode. Some of those load fine through the game's own menus ([manual mode](/guides/install-community-maps/#when-a-map-wont-load)). The full status list is on [/maps/](/maps/).

skatemods doesn't host these packs unless their authors uploaded them. Get them from the authors or the Discords linked above.

## Maps that boot

### San Vanelona spots

Eighteen spots from skate 2's San Vanelona, ported into Skate 3: the Art Gallery, Cathedral, City Hall, DIY Park, Mega Park, Monument, PJ Mansion, Slappys, Zen Plaza and more. Every one boots. The skate 2 city port started in 2023 with tuukkas, GHFear, wispp and others ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9-s0DX1-RKo)). The [Skate 3 history](/history/skate-3/) covers it.

### Meebs and Brassy ports

A work-in-progress collection of park ports. Three boot:

- **Auburn** by Lock
- **Boneyard** by Simpomode
- **Momentum 5.0** by DH13

Back SOV (Validus), Industrial Zone (Jean Olive) and The Alley (Ovanny) stall in automatic mode.

### EA's own DLC

If you own the Xbox 360 DLC, these all boot: **Danny Way's Mega Compound**, **Maloof Money Cup NYC**, the **San Van Party Pack** (Anti-Gravity Park) and **After Dark** (the Sanatorium). On the Recomp, put your own DLC package files in a `dlc` folder ([how](/guides/skate-3-recomp/#dlc)).

### Kenney Park

An original park built from Kenney's CC0 Mini Skate kit, and the first map uploaded to [skatemods](/maps/). Because it's an original, it's available converted for every platform, and it plays in the browser in [Clean Room Skate Online](/skate-3-online/) with no game files.

### GTA V Mega Brainrot

A meme map that boots. It's in the catalog as `gtarot`.

## Worth trying, untested

These are in the catalog but haven't been load-tested yet:

- **Skate It** locations: Barcelona, London, Paris, Rio, San Francisco, Shanghai, the Spillway and more
- **Venice Beach** (standalone)
- **Jump City** and **The Pit**, long jump lines with checkpoints
- **Ruthless Hill**, **Riverside DH**, **Blackwood DH** and other downhill runs

## Known to stall

The Sleepen Map Archive (Bay Creek, Harrisburgh, The Animal House, The Old Warehouse, X-Games Osaka 2025), Olay Holes, Rooftop Alley, Weining Lu and DHS by DH13 hang in automatic mode. Try [manual mode](/guides/install-community-maps/#when-a-map-wont-load) before giving up on them.

## Install

- **Recomp (PC, Steam Deck, Mac):** import the pack with the [Level Loader](/guides/install-community-maps/) and click the map.
- **RPCS3:** copy the map into your game's `USRDIR` as its readme says ([Skate 3 on RPCS3](/guides/skate-3-on-rpcs3/#dlc-and-custom-maps)).
- **PS3-only map on the Recomp:** [convert it](/guides/convert-ps3-maps/) first.
- **Real PS3:** a console on HEN or custom firmware takes custom maps the same way as RPCS3. A stock console can't load them. See the [real hardware FAQ](/faq/real-hardware/).
- **Browser:** maps converted to `.skate` play in [Clean Room Skate Online](/skate-3-online/) and the [Rust engine](/guides/skate-maps-rust-engine/).

## Skate 3 maps in skate. (2025)

Port Carverton itself is now a map for EA's newer game through ReSkate. [Skate 3 Improved](/reskate/333/Skate_3_Improved/) adds the DLC maps, and the [ReSkate maps list](/reskate/maps/) has the rest.

## Made a map?

[Upload it](/upload/). Once it's reviewed, skatemods converts it automatically (PS3 maps to recomp and `.skate`), and it shows up on [/maps/](/maps/) and in Clean Room Skate Online.
