*Session* went through years of early access before its full release as **Session: Skate Sim** in 2022. Like Skater XL, it's a realism-first sim with one stick per foot, but it's built on **Unreal Engine 4**.

## Unofficial but healthy

Crea-ture has never officially supported mods, so any game update can break them. The community built the tooling anyway:

- **Session Mod Manager** (rodriada000, open source) installs maps and textures and switches maps while the game is running.
- The **Illusory Mod Unlocker** patch lets the game load custom maps and textures at all. The Mod Manager downloads it for you.
- The **Illusory Discord** is the main place maps and assets are shared.

Steam Deck players have a [separate guide](https://hackmd.io/@agentender/H102AjAro) for modding through Proton.
