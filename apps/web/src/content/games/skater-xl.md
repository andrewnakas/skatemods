Skater XL entered early access on PC on **December 19, 2018** and fully launched on **July 28, 2020** for PC, PS4 and Xbox One, with Switch after. It uses a two-stick control scheme: each stick is one foot.

## Built for maps

Easy Day leaned into community content early. Community-made levels **shipped with the console release**, and Skater XL was one of the first games to support user mods on Nintendo Switch. Today **mod.io** is the central hub: real-world spot recreations, original parks, gear, and script mods. Game updates that changed lighting have broken maps before, and mappers fixed and re-released them.

## Multiplayer came from modders

Before official multiplayer existed, **silentbaws' XLMultiplayer** mod let people skate together, with replay support. When Easy Day released free official multiplayer for up to 10 players, XLMultiplayer was retired at the end of May 2021.
