The first **skate.** came out in September 2007 and replaced button-mash combos with **flick-it**: the right stick is your feet. It was built by EA Black Box, the team that went on to make skate 2 and Skate 3, and it was set in the fictional city of San Vanelona.

## Release and reception

| | |
|---|---|
| Xbox 360 | September 14, 2007 (North America), September 28 (PAL) |
| PlayStation 3 | September 24, 2007 (North America), October 5 (Europe) |
| Mobile | September 13, 2007, by EA Mobile |
| Metacritic | 86 (Xbox 360), 85 (PS3) |

Flick-it reads the analog sticks at 120 Hz, and the skater's body runs on a RenderWare physics package called Drives ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skate_(2007_video_game))). That physics-first approach carried through the whole series and into the 2025 [skate.](/games/skate-2025/).

## How to play it today

- **Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S.** skate. joined Xbox backward compatibility in June 2019, so the disc or digital Xbox 360 copy runs on current Xbox hardware.
- **Online is gone.** EA shut down the game's multiplayer in July 2011 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skate_(2007_video_game))). There's no community server for skate. 1 the way [Skate 3 has one](/faq/skate-3-online/).
- **Emulators.** Dump your own copy and run it on [RPCS3](/faq/rpcs3-basics/) for the PS3 version or [Xenia](/faq/xenia-basics/) for the Xbox 360 version. [Dumping your own games](/faq/dumping-your-own-games/) covers how.

## Modding today

The original has the smallest modding scene of the three console games. What exists is mostly:

- **Emulation** on RPCS3 and Xenia, which most modern mods build on.
- **Console trainers and RTE tools** for RGH/JTAG Xbox 360s, often bundled with skate 2 and 3 support, such as the JRPC/XDRPC [Skate mod tool](https://www.se7ensins.com/forums/threads/skate-mod-tool-jrpc-xdrpc.1531417/).

Its file formats are close to [skate 2](/games/skate-2/)'s and [Skate 3](/games/skate-3/)'s, so the reverse engineering done for Skate 3 (stream files, RenderWare 4 arenas, collision) is the most likely way into skate. 1 modding. The [Skate 3 modding history](/history/skate-3/) shows how far that work has come, from console trainers to a native PC port and a [map converter](/convert/).

If you're working on skate. 1, [tell us](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skatemods/issues) and we'll document it here.
