The first **skate.** came out in September 2007 and replaced button-mash combos with **flick-it**: the right stick is your feet. It was set in San Vanelona and built by the EA Black Box team that went on to make skate 2 and Skate 3.

## Modding today

The original has the smallest modding scene of the three. What exists is mostly:

- **Emulation** on RPCS3 and Xenia, which modern mods build on.
- **Console trainers and RTE tools** for RGH/JTAG Xbox 360s, often bundled with skate 2 and 3 support.

Its formats are close to skate 2's and Skate 3's, so the reverse engineering done for Skate 3 (stream files, RenderWare 4 arenas, collision) is the most likely way into skate. modding. If you're working on it, [tell us](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skatemods/issues) and we'll document it here.
