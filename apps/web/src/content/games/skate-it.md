**Skate It** brought the skate series to Nintendo hardware and phones. It's set between the first two games, in a San Vanelona wrecked by earthquakes, and later levels move to London, Barcelona, Paris, Shanghai, San Francisco and Rio de Janeiro ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skate_It)).

## Versions

| Platform | Released | Developer | Metacritic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wii | November 19, 2008 (NA), February 12, 2009 (Japan) | EA Montreal with EA Black Box | 70 |
| Nintendo DS | November 19, 2008 (NA) | Exient Entertainment | 72 |
| iOS | May 10, 2010 | not credited separately | 67 |

On Wii you steered with the Wii Remote, added a Nunchuk for finer steering and spins, or stood on the **Wii Balance Board**. On DS and iOS you flicked tricks with the stylus or a finger, a touch take on the [flick-it](/games/skate/) controls from the console games. IGN found the Wii controls took practice but gave a "nuanced trick system" ([via Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skate_It)).

## How to play it today

- **Wii on [Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org/download/).** The emulator runs Wii games on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android, and it can use a real Wii Remote over Bluetooth. Dump your own disc first ([how](/faq/dumping-your-own-games/)).
- **DS on an emulator** such as melonDS, from your own cartridge dump.
- **iOS** builds from 2010 don't run on current iPhones, which dropped 32-bit apps in iOS 11.

## Modding today

Skate It has almost no modding scene. The practical options are Dolphin's own features:

- **Custom texture packs.** Dolphin loads high-resolution replacement textures from a folder, so texture packs work without touching the game files.
- **Widescreen and resolution.** Dolphin renders above the Wii's 480p, and Gecko or AR codes can add widescreen hacks where the game lacks them.

Nobody has documented Skate It's file formats in public. Since it shares its setting and some design with skate. and [skate 2](/games/skate-2/), work on those games' formats may help. If you're modding it, [tell us](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skatemods/issues).

For the rest of the series, start with [Skate 3](/games/skate-3/), the most modded skate game, or [skate.](/games/skate-2025/) (2025) with [ReSkate](/reskate/).
