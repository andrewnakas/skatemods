**Skate Style** is from Zellah Games, run by Daniel Zeller, one of the developers behind the mobile hit *Skate City*. It was announced on **February 5, 2026**. Unusually for a skate game, it promises **full modding support** from the start, as "a platform for creativity, community, and self-expression". It has physics-based tricks, custom animations, deep character creation, a replay and clip editor, and maps based on Barcelona and Prague.

A demo is on Steam. skatemods will cover its modding tools when they ship.
