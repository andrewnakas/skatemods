**Skate Style** is from Zellah Games, run by Daniel Zeller, one of the developers behind the mobile hit *Skate City*. It was announced on **February 5, 2026** for PC, and its pitch puts modding up front: the game is meant as "a platform for creativity, community, and self-expression", with full modding support from the start ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/02/zellah-games-revealed-the-next-gen-modding-friendly-skate-style-for-pc/)).

## What's announced

- **Dual-stick controls** and a physics-based trick system, closer to [Skater XL](/games/skater-xl/) and [Session](/games/session/) than to flick-it.
- **A built-in animation editor**, so you can change how your skater moves, not only how they look ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sports/youve-had-skate-story-now-get-ready-for-skate-style-a-next-gen-skateboarding-game-that-lets-you-customise-your-characters-animations/)).
- **A character creator**, plus a replay and clip editor.
- **Maps based on real spots** in Barcelona and Prague.
- **Ray tracing** on high-end PCs. The studio says it runs at 40 to 60 frames per second on the Steam Deck at the lowest settings ([GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/02/zellah-games-revealed-the-next-gen-modding-friendly-skate-style-for-pc/)).

## Can you play it?

A free **[demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4369310/)** has been on Steam since February. The [full game's Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3862790/) lists Windows only, and as of October 10, 2026 the release date is "To be announced".

## Modding

No mod tools have shipped yet. The demo doesn't include them, and Zellah hasn't said what format mods will take or whether they'll go through Steam Workshop, mod.io or something else. That makes Skate Style the only upcoming skate game that plans for mods from day one. The others got them later: Skater XL added an official mod browser after launch, Session's mods are still unofficial, and skate. (2025) only has [ReSkate](/reskate/), a community project.
