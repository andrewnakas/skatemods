*Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3 + 4* came out in **July 2025** on every current platform, including Switch 2. Its biggest built-in creative tool is the upgraded **Create-A-Park**: dozens of objects, dynamic pieces, custom goals, and an online browser for player parks that you can load into private multiplayer matches.

## PC mods

On PC, the scene arrived within weeks of launch. Nexus Mods hosts custom characters, such as crossover skaters from other franchises, along with visual tweaks and a port of **THPSPro** with classic skaters, a free-roam camera and hidden cheats.

Mods are PC-only. The console versions only get Create-A-Park.
