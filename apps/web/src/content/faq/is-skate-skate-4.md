## Skate 4 and skate.

EA revealed a new skate game at EA Play Live 2020, and fans called it **Skate 4** from day one. In July 2022 EA named it **skate.**, lower case with a full stop like the 2007 original ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skate_(2025_video_game))). It entered early access on **September 16, 2025**, free-to-play on PC, PlayStation and Xbox. So "Skate 4 mods", "skate. mods" and "skate 2025 mods" all mean the same thing. See the [skate. game page](/games/skate-2025/) for the details.

## Modding it

The live game is online-only and protected by EA's Javelin anti-cheat, so mods can't touch it. [ReSkate](/history/reskate/), released October 2, 2026, works around that. Its launcher runs one fixed Steam build of skate. offline with a community runtime, which adds:

- custom maps, among them the full [Skate 3 city](/reskate/333/Skate_3_Improved/) and skate 2's [New San Vanelona](/reskate/brassy/Skate2Map/);
- cosmetics, soundtracks and scripts from [over 200 mods](/reskate/);
- a park editor and a built-in trainer;
- its own Steam lobbies and dedicated servers.

To set it up, follow the [ReSkate install guide](/guides/play-skate-with-reskate/). You need your own copy of [skate. on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3354750/), which is free.
