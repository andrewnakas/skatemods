## PS3 (for RPCS3)

[RPCS3](/faq/rpcs3-basics/)'s [quickstart](https://rpcs3.net/quickstart) lists two ways: "an original PlayStation 3 console with custom firmware or a PC with a compatible Blu-ray disc drive". On a console, homebrew file managers such as ManagunZ, IRISMAN or MultiMAN copy the game "via a custom or hybrid firmware". On PC, RPCS3's Disc Dumper is "an automated and user-friendly way of getting a decrypted copy of your PlayStation 3 discs". RPCS3 also needs the PS3 system software (`PS3UPDAT.PUP`), which you install through **File > Install Firmware**.

## Xbox 360 (for Xenia and skate3recomp)

[Xenia](/faq/xenia-basics/)'s [quickstart](https://github.com/xenia-canary/xenia-canary/wiki/Quickstart) is blunt: "YOU CANNOT PLAY GAMES DIRECTLY FROM THE DISC DRIVE, OR USE AN XBOX ONE/SERIES TO RIP GAMES!" The usual method works on a **stock, unmodded Xbox 360**: install the disc to the console's hard drive, then copy it off to USB. Redump-compatible PC drives are the other option. [skate3recomp](/guides/play-skate-3-on-pc/) asks you to "select your legally obtained copy of Skate 3" as an ISO, and DLC has to come from "your own legally obtained Xbox 360 DLC" ([README](https://github.com/mchughalex/skate3recomp)).

## Don't ask for downloads

The emulator communities don't allow it. RPCS3: "We do not condone piracy of any kind. Asking for, providing or discussing illegal download links is not allowed in our communities." skatemods follows the same rule: the [upload policy](/policy/) refuses retail districts and DLC.
