Session came out on PC, PlayStation 4 and 5, and Xbox One and Series X|S in September 2022, and on Nintendo Switch in March 2023 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Session:_Skate_Sim)). Only the PC version can be modded.

## Why it's PC only

Session's mods are unofficial. crea-ture told players before early access that official tools would come "eventually" ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/861650/discussions/0/2763442118821365907/)), and they haven't shipped. On PC, [Session Mod Manager](https://github.com/rodriada000/SessionMapSwitcher) and the [Illusory unlocker](https://illusory.dev/) patch the game so it loads custom maps and textures. Console versions can't be patched like that, and there is no in-game mod browser to fall back on.

## If you want custom maps on console

[Skater XL](/games/skater-xl/) is the skate sim with official mods. Its in-game Mod Browser serves maps and gear on PlayStation and Xbox as well as PC ([compare the two](/faq/skater-xl-vs-session/)).

On PC, Session mods run on Windows, Linux and Steam Deck. See [Session mods in 2026](/guides/session-mods/).
