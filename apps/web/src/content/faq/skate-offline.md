## The live game

EA's skate. is a free-to-play live-service game, and it needs EA's servers and can't be modded. [ReSkate](/history/reskate/), released October 2, 2026, is the way to play it offline. PC Gamer called it skate. being ["de-live serviced"](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sports/reskate-modding-platform-released/), and reported 350,000 downloads.

## How ReSkate does it

- It runs **one pinned Steam build** of skate. (build `25414733` as of 1.1.1), with a community runtime instead of EA's online services. It doesn't touch EA's live game or servers.
- Your settings and saves live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ReSkate\`, apart from the normal game's.
- You still need your own copy of [skate. on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3354750/). It's free, so adding it to your library is enough.
- **Steam signed in** gives you ReSkate's own multiplayer: Steam lobbies and community dedicated servers. **Steam closed** starts offline mode as "Unknown Player".

Offline you also get the things the live game doesn't have: [custom maps](/reskate/maps/), a park editor and a built-in trainer.

## Set it up

Follow the [ReSkate install guide](/guides/play-skate-with-reskate/). It takes the launcher zip from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Dingo-Shenanigans/ReSkate/releases/latest) and about 14 GB if the launcher has to download the game build for you. Windows Defender may flag the download; [Is ReSkate safe?](/faq/is-reskate-safe/) explains why.
