*This isn't legal advice, and Activision can change its rules or add anti-cheat at any time.*

## What the rules say

Activision's [terms of use](https://www.activision.com/legal/terms-of-use) prohibit using, developing, hosting or distributing "cheats, automation software (bots), modded lobbies, hacks, mods or any other unauthorized third-party software in connection with the Product". That covers both remakes.

## What actually happens

Neither game lists an anti-cheat system on [PCGamingWiki](https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Tony_Hawk's_Pro_Skater_3_+_4), and we found no reports of players banned for mods. *THPS 3+4* has eight-player cross-platform online through an Activision account ([Activision FAQ](https://support.activision.com/tony-hawks-pro-skater-3-4/articles/tony-hawks-pro-skater-3-4-faq)), so other players can see what your skater looks like.

Mod authors are cautious too. [THPSPro for 3+4](https://www.nexusmods.com/tonyhawksproskater34/mods/1) says "USE AT OWN RISK! – especially online with Skater Mods". [THPSPro for 1+2](https://www.nexusmods.com/tonyhawksproskater1and2/mods/121) has its own LAN/VPN free skate mode, and you remove `THPSPROonline_99_P.pak` to go back to official online.

## Rule of thumb

| You want | Do |
|---|---|
| Mods in single player | Fine in practice. Install them in `Base\Content\Paks\~mods`. |
| Online with friends | THPSPro's LAN/VPN free skate mode in 1+2. |
| Online with strangers | Move your `.pak` mods out of `~mods` first. |
