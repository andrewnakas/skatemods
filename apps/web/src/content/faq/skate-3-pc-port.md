## skate3recomp

[mchughalex/skate3recomp](https://github.com/mchughalex/skate3recomp) is "an unofficial native recompilation of the Xbox 360 version of Skate 3, supporting Windows, Linux, and macOS." The first release came out in June 2026, and v2.0 (July 24, 2026) added a native Direct3D 12 and Vulkan renderer. The repository has over 1,500 stars.

It's a recompilation, not an emulator: the game's code was translated to native code ahead of time ([how that works](/faq/recomp-vs-emulator/)). That's why people call it the Skate 3 PC port.

## What you need

- Your own **Xbox 360 Skate 3 disc image**. The project "does not include Skate 3 retail game files". See [Dumping your own games](/faq/dumping-your-own-games/).
- The [latest release](https://github.com/mchughalex/skate3recomp/releases/latest) for your system.

[Install it step by step](/guides/play-skate-3-on-pc/), or read the [full Recomp guide](/guides/skate-3-recomp/) for DLC, mods, Steam Deck and settings.

## Ports built on it

The same recompilation runs on Android ([Skate3-Mobile](https://github.com/Buku313/Skate3-Mobile)), on iPhone and iPad ([skate3-ios-setup](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skate3-ios-setup)) and on jailbroken PS4s ([skate3-ps4](https://github.com/OnlyMoisties/skate3-ps4)). Every one uses your own disc image.

## Downloads that include the game

Sites offering a "Skate 3 PC download" with the game files included aren't connected to this project. They're not legal to share and some carry malware. The real port is only on GitHub.
