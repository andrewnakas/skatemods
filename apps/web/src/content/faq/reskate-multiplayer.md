## Lobbies and servers

ReSkate has its own multiplayer, separate from EA's servers ([install guide](/guides/play-skate-with-reskate/#skate-with-friends)):

- **Steam lobby.** From the Multiplayer menu, host a lobby for up to 32 players. Make it public, or share its code, with an optional password. Lobbies have parties: invite, join, leave, kick and promote, with `/p` for party chat.
- **Dedicated servers.** **Multiplayer → Servers** lists community servers. To run one yourself, on Windows or natively on Linux, see [Host a ReSkate server](/guides/host-a-reskate-server/).

In a session you get proximity voice, text chat (**T**), throwdowns (Jam, Spot Battle and S.K.A.T.E.) and co-op challenges.

## What has to match

- **Steam must be running and signed in** when you press PLAY. Otherwise ReSkate starts offline as "Unknown Player".
- **Same ReSkate version** as the lobby or server. The launcher updates itself on start.
- **Same map mods** as the server when it's running a [custom map](/reskate/maps/).
- **Physics settings.** Under enforced physics, a host's [trainer](/guides/play-skate-with-reskate/#practice-with-the-trainer) setup reaches guests and they can't change their own.

## Bans

Since 1.1.1, lobbies and most dedicated servers turn away players on ReSkate's global ban list. That list is the ReSkate team's. ReSkate's own FAQ says EA can't ban you for using it.
