## Buying Skate 3

The [Xbox store](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/skate-3/bnkdkqxmxrr2) lists Skate 3 from $19.99 for Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S through backward compatibility. It's also included with **EA Play**, and Game Pass Ultimate can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming. Used Xbox 360 and PS3 discs are easy to find.

## Free ways to play

- **[Clean Room Skate Online](/skate-3-online/)**: a fan-made Skate 3 engine rewrite in your browser, with regenerated art and sound. Free, no download, no game files. It has parks and community maps, not the Skate 3 city.
- **[Skate 3 Recomp](/guides/skate-3-recomp/), RPCS3 and Xenia** are free software, but each needs a dump of a disc you own. [Every way to play Skate 3](/guides/play-skate-3-anywhere/) compares them.

## skate. (2025)

EA's [skate.](/games/skate-2025/), which fans call Skate 4, is free-to-play on Steam and consoles. It's a different game from Skate 3. [ReSkate](/guides/play-skate-with-reskate/) adds mods and offline play to it, and Skate 3's city is available there as a [map mod](/reskate/maps/).
