## THUG Pro

"The ultimate community mod for Tony Hawk's Underground 2, with multiplayer gameplay, user-created content, and levels from your favorite THPS games" ([thugpro.com](https://thugpro.com/)). It runs on THUG2's engine, so every level plays with THUG2's moveset. It's the place for online sessions with a big community and for custom levels. [History →](/history/thug-pro/)

## PARTYMOD

"A series of patches, mostly for the Tony Hawk's Pro Skater series, that fixes bugs as well as introduces modernizations such as modern controller support" ([partymod.newnet.city](https://partymod.newnet.city/)). There's one patch per game: THPS2, THPS3, THPS4, THUG, THUG2, American Wasteland and Mat Hoffman's Pro BMX. Each keeps that game as it was, and several reconnect it to [OpenSpy](https://openspy.net/) for online play. [History →](/history/partymod/)

## Rule of thumb

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| Every level in one game, online lobbies, custom parks | THUG Pro |
| THPS3 or THPS4 exactly as it was, just working on a modern PC | PARTYMOD for that game |
| Both | Install both. THUG Pro needs THUG2, and PARTYMOD patches each game separately. |

Setup steps are in [Get started with THUG Pro and PARTYMOD](/guides/thug-pro-and-partymod/).
