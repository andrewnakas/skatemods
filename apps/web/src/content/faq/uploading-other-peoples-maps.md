## What you can upload

From the [upload policy](/policy/):

- **Maps you made.** Your own geometry, layout and textures, built with community tools.
- **Maps someone else made, with their permission.** Say who granted it and where: a link to their post, a message, or a license that allows redistribution. Moderators may ask for proof.
- **Conversions of either.** Credit the original author in the "Original author" field.

## What you can't

- Maps you don't have permission for, even with credit. "I found it on YouTube" is not permission.
- Retail content, including "modified" retail districts. An edited University is still EA's University.
- Executables or scripts of any kind.

## If your map was uploaded without permission

Every map has a **Report** button that works without an account. Choose "This is my work", say where the original is and leave a contact. Credible claims are removed first and reviewed after ([takedowns](/policy/#takedowns)).

## Licenses

When you upload, you pick a license: CC BY 4.0, CC BY-NC 4.0, CC BY-ND 4.0, CC0, or "free to share unmodified with credit". It covers your own work only. Skate 3 itself, its formats and its trademarks belong to EA.
