## Modders first

Skater XL entered [early access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skater_XL) in December 2018 as a Unity game with a physics-driven board and little else, so players filled in the rest. Within days there were mods on GitHub: DanielKIWI's [replay and session tools](https://github.com/DanielKIWI/SkaterXL-Modding), then blendermf's [XLShredLoader](https://github.com/blendermf/XLShredLoader) on Unity Mod Manager. In July 2019 silentbaws shipped [XLMultiplayer](https://github.com/silentbaws/XLMultiplayer), and players skated together for almost two years before an official mode existed. Map makers built parks and street spots in Unity and passed them around Discord, using templates such as billowper's [Bills Tools](https://github.com/billowper/SXL-Template-Project) and, later, the community [mapping wiki](https://github.com/SkaterXLModding/skater-xl-mapping-wiki).

## The studio leans in

Easy Day Studios embraced the scene more fully than most. Before 1.0, it [announced](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/3444639924512737225) that three of the most popular community maps would ship in the game: Jean-Olive's "Streets", Pactole's "Hudland" and a map by Theo. It also said it had hired map creator Theo and programmer Kiwi, creator of the replay editor, as official team members. Through the scene, the studio said, it had found people with "a unique blend of both skateboarding and game development knowledge that might have been impossible to find through traditional means."

The same post pointed out a problem: in Discord, a creator's "hard work can get buried just days after it's been launched." The fix came in [update 1.1](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/3917534300074086855), a mod.io-powered browser in the main menu on PC, PlayStation and Xbox. Console players could finally get community maps and gear, and the browser passed [a million downloads in a single day](https://www.dualshockers.com/skater-xl-hits-over-1-million-mods-in-day-through-modding-platform/). Script mods stayed PC-only. Gear makers kept pushing with MCBTay's [XLGearModifier](https://github.com/MCBTay/XLGearModifier), which adds new meshes to the gear menu.

## Official multiplayer

In April 2021 Easy Day opened an [official multiplayer beta](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/4117954073003138582), and silentbaws retired XLMultiplayer: "With the release of official multiplayer there's no longer a space for the mod" ([README](https://github.com/silentbaws/XLMultiplayer)). A [DIY Map Editor](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/3936699406873695296) followed. Easy Day still features community maps by name in the browser, from Yaky's and FermenterGames' parks to Brookzee99's Oshawa Arena, home of the first FakeSkate Open. To start playing, see [Custom maps in Skater XL and Session](/guides/sim-custom-maps/).
