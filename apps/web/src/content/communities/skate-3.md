## Console-only, so modded consoles

Skate 3 shipped in 2010 on PS3 and Xbox 360 only, and EA Black Box closed in 2013. For years, modding it meant modding the console. On RGH/JTAG Xbox 360s that meant real-time editing: NDx's [Skate mod tool](https://www.se7ensins.com/forums/threads/skate-mod-tool-jrpc-xdrpc.1531417/) (2016) worked across all three skate games, Cult Crew [published the offsets](https://www.se7ensins.com/forums/threads/new-skate-3-offset-release.1673056/) for anyone to build on, and later [Skate3RTE](https://github.com/Jack06WS/Skate3RTE) went open source. On jailbroken PS3s it meant SPRX menus, like the one the EA SKATE MODDING Discord team built and Grim Doe packaged into a [one-click installer](http://web.archive.org/web/20251229022405/https://www.psx-place.com/threads/skate-3-all-in-one-modding-tool.38689/). Much of that era lived in Discords and videos that no longer exist, so it's the least documented part of this history.

## The emulator years

RPCS3 opened the game to anyone with a PC and their own copy. GHFear's [2019 walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JG-TRIlTzpQ) came out of "months of work" on EA's texture and model formats. Shellywell123 built the next layer: a [custom park swapper](https://github.com/Shellywell123/RPCS3_Skate3_Custom_Parks_Swapper) for players trading parks as save files, then [Skate 3 Texture Tools](https://github.com/Shellywell123/Skate-3-Texture-Tools), which credits "GHFear's Youtube for the initial manual tutorial!" Others added a trainer ([CH3AT](https://github.com/ckosmic/CH3AT)), board graphics ([S3GE](https://github.com/devHazz/s3ge)), and GHFear's [RW4ArchiveTool](https://github.com/GHFear/RW4ArchiveTool) for the game's archives.

In June 2020 the **EA Skate Modding** Discord opened, and it became the scene's home for years. It passed 86,000 members by 2026 ([invite](https://discord.com/invite/aQxCHpdH2M)). wispp released the [S3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2JMoR7x5XDE) mod menu there in 2021, then the [Native Menu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EuQUM_Ez2cw). The Native Menu is drawn by the game itself, and it became a widely used way to load mods on RPCS3. The Mad Man's [ragdoll mod](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ewxUfeiz2NY) went viral. In 2023, tuukkas, GHFear, wispp and others brought [Skate 2's San Vanelona](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9-s0DX1-RKo) into Skate 3, followed by its DLC maps, years before the world format was fully writable. Meanwhile the [Skate Modding Team](https://github.com/Skate-Modding-Team) started documenting the file formats.

Getting back online took the longest. [Skateboard3Server](https://github.com/hallofmeat/Skateboard3Server) got as far as authentication in 2020. In June 2025, wispp's proxy got RPCS3 players [online for the first time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yxnVyy8E8zc), and a Skate RPCS3 Online Discord grew past 10,000 members. In 2026 wispp followed it with a [full Blaze server](https://github.com/skate6743/Skate3BlazeServer). It built on Aim4Kill's BlazeSDK, gamingrobot's TDF documentation and Tratos's Blaze emulator, and BWKingsnake made a config tool so players could connect. skate6743's [Arcadia fork](https://github.com/skate6743/arcadia-skate) did the same for skate and skate 2.

## The Discord and the world format

In September 2025 the map-making effort got its own server, the **Dumbads & SunJays Skate3 Modding Discord** ([invite](https://discord.com/invite/AgDEQFR2Jj)). The shared toolset is credited to "[SunJay, Dumbad, Tuukkas, Wissp and S4M](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skate3-ps3-to-x360)". Dumbads' [ArenaBuilder and DlcBuilder](https://github.com/Ethanw05/DumbadsSkate3ModdingTools) made the world format writable, so custom maps could ship through the old DLC slots. SunJay's [GLB 2 ARENA](https://github.com/SunJaycy/sk83.GLB2ARENA) turns any 3D model into a game-ready mesh. ChallengeEditor lets maps ship with their own Game of S.K.A.T.E. and races. The PS3 → recomp importer is, in its own words, "the work of several people in the Dumbads & SunJays Skate3 Modding Discord" ([README](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skate3-ps3-map-importer)), and randyadr's GUI importer opened it to everyone. By early 2026 YouTubers were calling the scene [better than ever](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1YfQpbHTZn8).

## Native, everywhere

In 2026, Alex McHugh released [skate3recomp](https://github.com/mchughalex/skate3recomp), a static recompilation of the Xbox 360 game into a native PC program, after portingpete's [first bring-up](https://github.com/portingpete/skate3-recomp). Within weeks, people were [asking for mod support](https://github.com/mchughalex/skate3recomp/issues/17). The recomp also became a base for other projects:
- Android builds by [Buku313](https://github.com/Buku313/Skate3-Mobile) and others
- a [jailbroken PS4 port](https://github.com/OnlyMoisties/skate3-ps4)
- iOS and web builds
- chasmlol's [SK8 Engine](https://github.com/SK8-ENGINE/SK8-Engine) and [Rust engine](https://github.com/SK8-ENGINE/skate-3-rust-engine), which brought a portable `.skate` map format and an [in-game maps browser](https://github.com/chasmlol/skate3-custom-maps-backend)

## Port Carverton in the new game

On October 2, 2026, the day [ReSkate](/history/reskate/) opened EA's 2025 skate. to mods, zeex64 put [the full Skate 3 map](https://thunderstore.io/c/reskate/p/zeex64/Full_Skate_3_Map/) on its Thunderstore. Port Carverton became the platform's most downloaded mod on day one, ahead of brassy's port of skate 2's [New San Vanelona](https://thunderstore.io/c/reskate/p/brassy/Skate2Map/). SunJay, who co-runs the Skate 3 modding Discord, shipped ReSkate mods that same day.

skatemods.com exists to tie these together. A map uploaded once converts to every platform, and every tool above is credited to the people who built it.
