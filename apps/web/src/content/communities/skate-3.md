## Console-only, so modded consoles

Skate 3 shipped in 2010 on PS3 and Xbox 360 only, and EA Black Box closed in 2013. For years, modding it meant modding the console. On RGH/JTAG Xbox 360s that meant real-time editing tools such as [Skate3RTE](https://github.com/Jack06WS/Skate3RTE). On jailbroken PS3s it meant SPRX menus, like the one the EA SKATE MODDING Discord team built and Grim Doe packaged into a [one-click installer](http://web.archive.org/web/20251229022405/https://www.psx-place.com/threads/skate-3-all-in-one-modding-tool.38689/). Much of that era lived in Discords and videos that no longer exist, so it's the least documented part of this history.

## The emulator years

RPCS3 opened the game to anyone with a PC and their own copy. GHFear's [2019 walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JG-TRIlTzpQ) came out of "months of work" on EA's texture and model formats. Shellywell123 built the next layer: a [custom park swapper](https://github.com/Shellywell123/RPCS3_Skate3_Custom_Parks_Swapper) for players trading parks as save files, then [Skate 3 Texture Tools](https://github.com/Shellywell123/Skate-3-Texture-Tools), which credits "GHFear's Youtube for the initial manual tutorial!" Others added a trainer ([CH3AT](https://github.com/ckosmic/CH3AT)), board graphics ([S3GE](https://github.com/devHazz/s3ge)), and GHFear's [RW4ArchiveTool](https://github.com/GHFear/RW4ArchiveTool) for the game's archives.

Getting back online took the longest. [Skateboard3Server](https://github.com/hallofmeat/Skateboard3Server) got as far as authentication in 2020. Then skate6743 built on Aim4Kill's BlazeSDK to release a [full Blaze server](https://github.com/skate6743/Skate3BlazeServer), and BWKingsnake made a config tool so players could connect. Matchmaking and Skate.Park sharing came back for RPCS3 players, and skate6743's [Arcadia fork](https://github.com/skate6743/arcadia-skate) did the same for skate and skate 2.

## The Discord and the world format

Most modern Skate 3 modding goes through the **Dumbads & SunJays Skate3 Modding Discord**. The shared toolset is credited to "[SunJay, Dumbad, Tuukkas, Wissp and S4M](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skate3-ps3-to-x360)". Dumbads' [ArenaBuilder and DlcBuilder](https://github.com/Ethanw05/DumbadsSkate3ModdingTools) made the world format writable, so custom maps could ship through the old DLC slots. SunJay's [GLB 2 ARENA](https://github.com/SunJaycy/sk83.GLB2ARENA) turns any 3D model into a game-ready mesh. ChallengeEditor lets maps ship with their own Game of S.K.A.T.E. and races. The PS3 → recomp importer is, in its own words, "the work of several people in the Dumbads & SunJays Skate3 Modding Discord" ([README](https://github.com/andrewnakas/skate3-ps3-map-importer)), and randyadr's GUI importer opened it to everyone. By early 2026 YouTubers were calling the scene [better than ever](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1YfQpbHTZn8).

## Native, everywhere

In 2026, Alex McHugh released [skate3recomp](https://github.com/mchughalex/skate3recomp), a static recompilation of the Xbox 360 game into a native PC program, after portingpete's [first bring-up](https://github.com/portingpete/skate3-recomp). Within weeks, people were [asking for mod support](https://github.com/mchughalex/skate3recomp/issues/17). The recomp also became a base for other projects:
- Android builds by [Buku313](https://github.com/Buku313/Skate3-Mobile) and others
- a [jailbroken PS4 port](https://github.com/OnlyMoisties/skate3-ps4)
- iOS and web builds
- chasmlol's [SK8 Engine](https://github.com/SK8-ENGINE/SK8-Engine) and [Rust engine](https://github.com/SK8-ENGINE/skate-3-rust-engine), which brought a portable `.skate` map format and an [in-game maps browser](https://github.com/chasmlol/skate3-custom-maps-backend)

skatemods.com exists to tie these together. A map uploaded once converts to every platform, and every tool above is credited to the people who built it.
