## THAW instead of THUG2

[THUG Pro](/history/thug-pro/) built its collection of every Neversoft level on *Tony Hawk's Underground 2*. reTHAWed does the same job on the next game, *American Wasteland*, so it plays with THAW's moves: walking, bert slides, Natas spins and the BMX bike. The [fan wiki](https://tonyhawkgames.fandom.com/wiki/ReTHAWed) puts the difference this way: "unlike THUG Pro, reTHAWed allows players to play through the entire THAW story campaign". Its levels come from every main Neversoft game from THPS1 to Project 8, plus Disney's Extreme Skate Adventure. Those include the Collector's Edition levels that Aspyr's [2006 PC port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Hawk%27s_American_Wasteland) left out. Online play goes through [OpenSpy](/history/partymod/), the replacement for GameSpy.

The mod is made by **10K Rising**, whose [Discord](https://discord.gg/rethawed) describes itself as "a modding community for the Tony Hawk's game series, primarily focused on THAW". The team overlaps with other Neversoft-engine projects. Zedek and Uzis are both credited on [Guitar Hero World Tour: Definitive Edition](https://ghwt.de/credits), which runs on a later version of the same engine. reTHAWed's own credits thank [PARTYMANX](/history/partymod/) for rewriting its input and output, [bAstimc](/history/thps-pro/) for THPS 1+2 physics, CHC for OpenSpy, and Neversoft co-founder Mick West for information about the engine.

## Release by release

The first beta came out on [October 8, 2017](https://tonyhawkgames.fandom.com/wiki/ReTHAWed). Releases since version 3.0 in March 2023 are in a [public GitGud repository](https://gitgud.io/10k-rising/rethawed-release-repository), and every commit message there is a full changelog. Uzis ran the early releases. Zedek has written nearly all of them since 4.1 in mid-2024, and the updates got much bigger:

- **[4.1](https://gitgud.io/10k-rising/rethawed-release-repository/-/commit/6b6e6d50abcbb62fbbabf15d5db029052b60d9be)** (June 2024) brought in levels from six games, a flip and roll system rewritten from scratch, Project 8 slow spins and ultrawide screens.
- **[4.2](https://gitgud.io/10k-rising/rethawed-release-repository/-/commit/951845fac89bdee3dfb4ebc481b73c3814b62752)** (Christmas 2024) added mod support. Skaters, decks, items and levels install as add-ons from the [thpsGoat depository](https://thpsgoat.com/mods), and custom levels are validated so they work online.
- **[4.3](https://gitgud.io/10k-rising/rethawed-release-repository/-/commit/f6844390d8a9ec4de56059a9aaba56a28ce1dcb0)** (May 2025) finished THPS4's level list.
- **[4.4](https://gitgud.io/10k-rising/rethawed-release-repository/-/commit/4583208bfa05cef5b4f9967a277ff4007b209c68)** (October 2025) added full classic mode tours for THPS3 and Project 8, with characters and decks unlocked by playing them.
- **[4.5](https://gitgud.io/10k-rising/rethawed-release-repository/-/commit/5265873532a743a3a7e39f4cacf37c405715ccb4)** (March 2026) added THPS2 and THUG2 classic modes. [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/rethawed-tony-hawks-american-wasteland-community-mod-releases-its-4-5-update-adds-thps2-and-thug2-levels-skaters-modes.1464871/) picked it up from a summary by TheNathanNS.

## 5.0

Version 5.0 came out on October 4, 2026, with a [launch trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YYs3k8Gy1W8). The biggest addition is the whole THUG2 story mode, playable from a new story menu next to THAW's. Our [5.0 write-up](/blog/rethawed-5/) covers the rest.

## Installing it

reTHAWed installs through the **reTHAWed Updater**, Zedek's [NX Mod Updater](https://gitgud.io/ZedekThePD/nx-mod-updater), for Windows or Linux. You need your own copy of THAW for PC. The game doesn't have to run, but its files must not be in `Program Files`. The download is about 7 GB, and the updater needs .NET Framework 4.8. The team notes that antivirus warnings about the unsigned updater are false positives, and you can build it from source if you'd rather. Steps are in [Get started with THUG Pro, reTHAWed and PARTYMOD](/guides/thug-pro-and-partymod/#rethawed-thaw-with-everything-in-it).
